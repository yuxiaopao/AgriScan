Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary brand logo for the existing Chinese agricultural crop health platform 禾观 AgriScan.

Symbol: invent a beautifully resolved compact emblem in which one fresh leaf is simultaneously the iris of a softly shaped observing eye. Integrate the forms into ONE coherent memorable geometric silhouette, with a simple clean negative-space leaf vein that subtly conveys precision and observation. Organic yet precise, friendly yet credible. Do not assemble multiple stock icons. Keep the mark bold enough to read at favicon scale. Flat vector-like artwork, exceptionally crisp edges, refined optical proportions.
Composition: a single horizontal logo lockup centered on a genuinely transparent canvas. Emblem on the left, generous but coherent spacing, on the right a strong Chinese wordmark 禾观 above a smaller Latin wordmark AgriScan. Wordmark rendering must be exact: 禾观 and AgriScan. The Chinese typography is contemporary and subtly rounded, medium-bold with careful spacing, professional and highly legible; Latin is refined humanist sans serif. Symbol height approximately equal to the complete two-line wordmark height. Artwork fills the useful center with ample transparent outer margin.
Color palette: forest green #2D704D as the dominant symbol and wordmark color, restrained fresh sage #91B77B accent within the leaf. Maximum two flat inks. No gradients.
Constraints: actual transparent background with alpha, no drawn checkerboard, no white or colored canvas rectangle, no mockup, no paper texture, no shadow, no 3D, no photo, no robot, no border frame, no extra slogan, no tiny explanatory text, no multiple alternatives, no watermark. Deliver one finished logo only.
